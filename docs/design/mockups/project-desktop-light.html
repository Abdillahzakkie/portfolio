Settleo

Non-custodial P2P / OTC crypto-settlement platform, built as ~24 independently deployable services.

SolidityGogRPCTigerBeetleRedis

Overview

Settleo settles peer-to-peer and OTC crypto trades without ever taking custody of user funds. The platform is split into around two dozen services — each its own repository — so the trust-critical parts stay small, auditable, and independently deployable.

An internet-facing gateway/BFF is the only public surface: it does HMAC and token authentication, deny-by-default authorization, rate limiting, idempotency, signed internal routing, and WebSocket fan-out, keeping its own state in Redis. Behind it, an IAM service issues and introspects sessions and tokens and enforces an AWS-IAM-style, deny-wins policy model.

The money moves through an authoritative double-entry ledger that is the sole writer to TigerBeetle and speaks gRPC to the rest of the system. A per-chain EVM indexer ingests on-chain events and credits balances in a reorg-safe way, while a 2-of-3 non-custodial escrow — Solidity contracts plus an orchestrator — drives fund, release, refund, and dispute flows and settles them back against the ledger.

Highlights

  • Deny-by-default gateway: HMAC + token auth, rate limiting, idempotency, signed routing.
  • Authoritative double-entry ledger as the single writer to TigerBeetle over gRPC.
  • 2-of-3 non-custodial trade escrow with an orchestrator for fund / release / refund / dispute.
  • Reorg-safe per-chain EVM indexing that credits balances only after confirmation.
  • Compliance and dispute services for KYC/KYB/AML and 2-of-3 arbitration.
